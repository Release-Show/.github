**Teams ship constantly, and almost nobody sees it.** Changelogs are walls of text, and a launch video
takes hours to make, so the work goes unnoticed by customers, investors and the rest of the company.

**release.show turns every release into a premiere.** Connect GitHub, your changelog and your website.
Ship as usual. release.show detects new releases, merged pull requests and site changes, and renders a
30 to 90 second video: branded, captioned and ready to post.

- **Videos from your commits.** Releases, PRs and commits become scenes, cut and timed automatically.
- **Website change detection.** Before and after captures of what changed on your site.
- **Your brand, every format.** Logo, colors and fonts on every video; 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 from one render.
- **Script, voice and avatar.** An AI script, optional voiceover or an AI avatar presenter, and captions you can edit.
- **One release, every format.** The same release also becomes a blog post, an in-app release widget,
  social posts and an email digest.
- **A channel per project.** `release.show/<project>`: one episode per release, plus an embed for your changelog.
